FAQ

Custom balloon questions, answered

What is the minimum order for custom balloons?

500 pcs per design for custom print and die. Stock designs can be mixed freely to reach any quantity.

How long does a custom sample take?

7 days after you approve the digital artwork proof. Express shipping adds 3–5 days to most markets.

Is there a tooling charge?

No tooling charge if your shape matches one of our 300+ stock dies. A custom die costs [your tooling price] once and stays reusable for all future orders.

Can you print multi-color gradients and photos?

Yes — we print digital-quality gradients and photo artwork on flat areas. Keep small text above 6 pt for crisp results.

Do custom balloons come with safety reports?

Yes. We test to EN71 (EU) and ASTM F963 / CPSIA (US). Reports are issued per project and available for download.

How is payment handled?

30% T/T deposit to start production, 70% balance against a copy of the bill of lading. PayPal or card accepted for sample orders.
