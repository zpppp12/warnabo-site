Compliance

Compliance documents, ready before you ask

Balloons sold in the EU and US are regulated as toys. We test our foil balloons to the major standards and publish the documents here — so your buyers' import checks never stall.

EN 71 (EU / UK)

Toys Safety Standard — Part 1 mechanical & physical, Part 2 flammability, Part 3 migration of certain elements. Required for EU and UK import.

ASTM F963 (US)

US Consumer Product Safety Commission toy standard. Our foil balloons are tested against the applicable sections.

CPSIA (US)

Children's product requirements including lead and phthalates limits, plus tracking labels on retail packaging.

MSDS

Material Safety Data Sheet for the foil laminate, inks and adhesives used in production.

REACH / SVHC

Compliance statements for chemical substances, commonly requested by EU corporate buyers.

Factory audit files

BSCI/SEDEX-style audit documentation available on request for buyers who require social compliance.

Frequently asked by compliance-minded buyers

Can I get the test reports before ordering?

Yes. Email us or message on WhatsApp and we will send the latest reports for the product lines you are considering — usually within the same day.

Are reports issued per design or per material?

Reports are issued per material and print process. If your custom artwork uses the same ink system and film, the existing report usually covers it — we confirm this before quoting.

Which reports do I need for my market?

EU/UK: EN 71. US: ASTM F963 + CPSIA. Middle East and most other markets have no mandatory toy testing, though many importers still ask for EN 71 as a quality benchmark. Tell us your destination and we will advise.

Do retail packs carry warning labels?

Yes. Balloons are a choking hazard for children under 8 — retail packaging includes the required warning text for US shipments (CPSIA tracking label applied on request).

Need a specific document?

Tell us the standard and product line. Reports are sent within one business day.
